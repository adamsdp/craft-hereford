CRAFT HEREFORD LOGO PACK
========================

Open Craft-Hereford-Logo-Guide.pdf first. It shows every version and when to use it.

FOLDERS
-------
01-badge             The main round logo
02-icon              Hands and heart only, for small spaces
03-horizontal        Icon + name side by side, for wide spaces
04-wordmark          The name on its own

  Each of these has:
    png/   See-through background. Small (500px), medium (1500px), large (3000px).
           Use these for Word, Canva, Facebook, Instagram and everyday things.
    jpg/   White background, for anywhere that won't accept PNG.
    pdf/   Sharp at any size. Send these to printers and sign makers.
    svg/   Sharp at any size. For websites and designers.

  Colour versions:
    colour                       Light backgrounds (the usual choice)
    white                        Dark backgrounds and photos
    blue / black                 One-colour printing, stamps, newsletters
    white-outline (badge)        Vinyl lettering on the glass cabinets
    for-dark-backgrounds         Horizontal logo on dark backgrounds, in colour

  The white versions look blank on a white screen. That's normal.

05-social-media      Ready to upload:
    profile-picture-icon-1080    Instagram / Facebook profile picture (recommended)
    profile-picture-badge-1080   Alternative profile picture with the full badge
    facebook-cover-1640x624      Facebook page cover photo
    link-preview-1200x630        The picture shown when the website is shared

06-website-icons     Browser tab and phone home-screen icons

07-email-signature   Logo for your email signature. Insert the 600px file
                     and set its width to 300px so it stays sharp.
