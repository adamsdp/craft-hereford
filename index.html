Made with love in Herefordshire

Handmade treasures, waiting for you with your coffee.

Craft Hereford is a volunteer-run community group supporting local crafters. Look out for our glass display cabinets in independent coffee shops across the county, each one full of work by makers who live just down the road.

Buying is easy

No queue, no fuss, no extra work for the café team.

  1. 1

    Spot a cabinet

    Find a Craft Hereford cabinet in one of our host coffee shops, then take a good look while you enjoy your drink.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code

    Seen something you love? Scan the QR code on the cabinet with your phone camera and pay securely online.

  3. 3

    Take home something handmade

    Every piece is made locally, so you're getting something one-of-a-kind and helping a Herefordshire maker too.
